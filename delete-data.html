Data Deletion Request

Medicine Scanner · Android application

Medicine Scanner is developed by Phwrman Brahma. You can contact us to request deletion of any personal data associated with your use of the app that we retain.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to phwrmanbrahma777@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Medicine Scanner - Data Deletion Request.
  3. Describe the information you want deleted and, if relevant, approximately when you used the app. Do not send medicine photographs, passwords, or sensitive medical information unless specifically necessary.

Email a deletion request

What data is handled

The app uses on-device text recognition to read medicine packaging. Extracted medicine text and details may be sent to the Medicine Scanner backend to organize the scan and retrieve medicine reference information. Based on the currently reviewed implementation, the backend does not intentionally create a permanent database of individual scans.

When you tap Save in the app, medicine details are saved for the current app session in memory. The app does not require an account.

What can be deleted or retained

We will review requests and delete any identifiable user information under our control that we can locate and are not required to retain. If no identifiable scan record is retained, we will explain that in our response. Information held only in the app's current session can be removed by closing the app; you can also clear the app's local data or uninstall it to remove locally stored app data. Clearing local app data may not remove images already saved in your device's photo library.

Limited technical or service-provider logs may be retained for security, reliability, or legal purposes according to applicable retention practices. We cannot promise immediate deletion of records controlled independently by service providers.

Response and questions

We will review your request and respond by email. We may ask for limited additional information if needed to locate relevant records, but please do not send sensitive medical information unnecessarily.

For privacy information, see the Medicine Scanner Privacy Policy.
